Gia Lai Aquatic Biodiversity Knowledge Graph

Ayun Ha Reservoir, Ialy Reservoir, Se San River and Bien Ho Lake. Data are read from gialai_biokg.ttl, produced by the BioKG pipeline from 10 source documents.

Click a node to select it; drag to move. presentAt edges are derived from SiteOccurrence nodes with status "present".

Schema

Each checklist row becomes a NameUsage node (the name as printed). It links to its source document, its waterbody and a dwc:Taxon identified by a GBIF IRI. Each conservation assessment is a separate node recording the scheme, its version and the asserting document. Abundance and water-quality values are sosa:Observation nodes attached to a sampling site and a survey campaign.

flowchart LR
  USE["NameUsage"] -- "prov:wasDerivedFrom" --> SRC["SourceDocument"]
  USE -- "bkg:resolvesTo" --> TAX["dwc:Taxon"]
  USE -- "bkg:recordedIn" --> WB["Waterbody"]
  USE -- "bkg:hasAssessment" --> ASS["ConservationAssessment"]
  ASS -- "bkg:assessedTaxon" --> TAX
  SITE["SamplingSite"] -- "bkg:inWaterbody" --> WB
  OCC["SiteOccurrence"] -- "bkg:ofUsage" --> USE
  OCC -- "bkg:atSite" --> SITE
  AB["AbundanceObservation"] -- "bkg:observedTaxon" --> TAX
  AB -- "sosa:hasFeatureOfInterest" --> SITE
  WQ["WaterQualityObservation"] -- "sosa:hasFeatureOfInterest" --> SITE
